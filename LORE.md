# Evil Fungus

There's only one. However many patches turn up, it's the same one, and when two of them touch they just kind of agree on that.

It's after carbon. People, animals, plants, anything that grew. Robots walk across it like carpet and it doesn't care, which is honestly the most insulting part.

It can't see. It feels you walking on it. It smells you breathing.

Nobody it takes gets hurt. They lie down on it and go to sleep, and they look like they're having a great time.

The air over it is really nice. Cleaner than outside. You'll catch yourself taking deep breaths, and that's fine, that's what it's for.

Someone put a scraping under a microscope once. Hexagons, all the way down.

## How that plays

| Lore | In game | |
|---|---|---|
| There's only one | Mats that touch merge into one body and one brain | done |
| After carbon | Takes players and NPCs that bleed, and digests plants, produce and bodies left on the mat. Nextbots and anything that doesn't bleed, or bleeds oil, walk over it unharmed | done |
| Nobody gets hurt | No damage. The body on you makes you drowsy, then you go under. The game counts it as a death, but your body stays on the mat, owned by the fungus: no pickup, no remover, no undo. It only goes when the fungus does | done |
| Lives off sleepers | Every body it keeps feeds it a little, forever, from their breathing | done |
| Snow angels | Braided arms rise off the mat and lay every sleeper out on their back, arms and legs spread, against whatever surface it's on. Floor, wall or ceiling. Nothing else happens until the pose is right | done |
| Only the brain | Over five minutes everything but the head is replaced with fungus, from the fingertips in. The shape stays. A vein runs from the neck into the mat | done |
| Tending | A fresh catch gets the body's full attention. It pools around them and the mat knits itself tight underneath | done |
| Wiring them in | Within seconds, fine fibers come up off the mat and feel around for the eyes, nose, mouth, ears and the other end, then take hold. More grab the skin. Over the whole consume, thicker strands get thrown over them until they're cocooned | done |
| Compute nodes | The head keeps working. Pale beads run down the neck vein into the mat, all the time | done |
| Getting someone back | For the first minute of that, a sleeper can be torn out by anything with enough force. Ten seconds later they get up. NPCs come back terrified of it and won't go near the mat for a while. Players stay held until then, unless the server lets them respawn | done |
| Feels you walking | Footsteps ripple through the mat to the body. Crouching is quiet, sprinting is loud, jumping over it is silent | done |
| Smells you breathing | Breathers leave a CO2 plume, heavier when running or hurt. It spreads up the plume toward you from off the mat | planned |
| Plants | Don't breathe out what it smells, so it only finds them by touch | done |
| The air is nice | It gives off oxygen. The lure. Standing on it should feel good enough that you stay | open |
| Hexagons | Look only, maybe a cell pattern in the mat | idea |
